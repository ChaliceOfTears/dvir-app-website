Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 22, 2026

DVIR Pro ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use our mobile application.

1. Data Collection and Usage

DVIR Pro is built with an offline-first architecture. All driver names, truck/trailer numbers, odometer readings, and defect notes created within the app are stored locally on your device using a local SQLite database.

2. In-App Purchases and Subscription Data

In-app purchase transactions and subscriptions are processed securely by Google Play Store Billing and RevenueCat. We do not collect, store, or have access to your credit card or financial billing information.

3. Speech Recognition and Camera Permissions

Voice-to-text features utilize your device's native speech recognition engine. Camera permissions are used strictly to attach defect photos to your inspection reports on-device.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal or inspection data to any third parties.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@dvirproapp.com
